# Earth to Mars research follow-up questions

Date: 2026-11-19 (America/New_York)

Status: Open questions for later research. No additional document acquisition or application implementation is authorized by this list.

Related note: [Crewed journey from Earth to Mars](2026-11-19-earth-to-mars-overview.md).

## Source verification

1. What are the exact titles, authors, dates, versions, and relevant pages of each linked source? Which claims are explicitly supported, and which are inference?
2. What is the formal name and scope of the 2024 notional architecture? How does it differ from the 2023 crew-operations study?
3. Which claims can the three existing local PDFs support? Verify their identities and editions before using them as evidence.

## Mission consistency

4. Which one mission concept should anchor a later worked example? Record crew size, propulsion, vehicles, departure year, transit duration, and surface stay together.
5. Does the six-to-nine-month range apply to that concept? What propulsion and trajectory assumptions determine it?
6. How do the roughly 26-month alignment cycle and an actual launch window differ? What would a missed window mean for this mission?
7. What cargo must arrive first, and which systems must be checked remotely before the crew departs? Is return propellant carried or produced at Mars?
8. Where are the vehicles assembled, checked, and fueled? How many launches and docking operations does the selected concept require?

## Crew and arrival

9. What life-support reserves, maintenance capability, exercise, radiation protection, and medical capability are assumed during transit?
10. How do communication delay and outages change through the selected trajectory, and which decisions must the crew or onboard systems make independently?
11. Does the crew enter Mars orbit or descend directly? What original source supports each alternative, and what are its capture, abort, and contingency assumptions?
12. What entry mass, thermal protection, aerodynamic braking, and powered-descent system does the selected lander require? What has been demonstrated and what remains proposed or simulated?
13. What landing precision and hazard-avoidance capability are required to reach prepositioned supplies, and what must happen immediately after touchdown?
